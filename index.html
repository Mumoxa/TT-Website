About Talent Tree

Built in South Africa. Ready for broader reach.

Talent Tree was established in 2013 with a simple belief: good recruitment should be sharp, credible and built on reputation.

Strong local reputation

We have spent more than a decade building our name in the South African market through consistent delivery, trusted relationships and focused work in niche hiring. Our clients range from startups to some of Africa’s largest Financial Services and Retail groups.

Preferred where precision matters

For a number of our clients, we are the preferred recruitment partner when specialist capability is hard to find and the quality of the search matters more than the size of the database.

International flavour, grounded delivery

Alongside our local strength, we support selected international hiring needs. We understand the practical reality of hiring across markets without losing the precision and care needed to make the right appointment.
