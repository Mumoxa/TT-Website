## Content to obtain privately

The approved five applied questions, four choices per question, correct choices,
three reconciling discrepancy references, corrective actions, corrected balance,
unsafe actions and signed supplier/ledger source records are not available in this
cloud checkout. Author them in the protected Assessment setup form, save a private
D1 draft, review it with an AP subject-matter expert, and publish only after approval.
The JSON field guide is in the form. Editing creates a new immutable draft; existing
attempts keep their assigned version. Approval requires both checkboxes and an
internal approval reference; the publication audit stores that reference.

Proposed topics for the client to develop (not approved exam questions): invoice
validation/three-way matching; duplicate invoice investigation; statement/ledger
reconciling items; payment controls and verified supplier bank changes; appropriate
period-end corrections. The practical records should contain three unambiguous
items with documented evidence and a uniquely derived corrected balance. Explain
signed amount conventions and confirm the six-column, twenty-row workbook is enough.
Pilot the complete ten-minute exercise with experienced AP staff. Provisional scoring
is 40 technical + 60 reconciliation, not a validated selection threshold.

Synthetic fixtures in tests are deliberately public test material. They must never
become the real SATURC assessment or be represented as approved content.

## Proposed candidate wording — requires human approval

“Talent Tree Consulting will use your first name, surname, email address, assessment
responses, spreadsheet workings, timing and assessment result to evaluate this
recruitment assignment. Authorised Talent Tree reviewers will review the result.
Any disclosure to SATURC, the lawful basis and the recipients must be explained in
the assignment-specific privacy notice supplied by your recruiter before you start.
Your entered details are self-declared and do not verify your identity. This short
work sample is one piece of screening evidence and does not establish full AP
competence. Contact your Talent Tree recruiter to request access, correction,
deletion, an accommodation or support. Please do not enter unrelated personal,
financial or confidential information in the worksheet.”

Confirm an approved privacy contact and accessible accommodation route before use.
The acknowledgement records receipt of a notice, not blanket consent or legal
approval. The current page requires candidates to confirm they received that notice.

## Retention proposal — not an implemented or approved policy

Proposed default for unsuccessful candidates: delete assessment identity, invitations,
attempts, workbook snapshots and results 90 days after the assignment closes, unless
an approved legal hold applies. Successful-candidate retention and employer copies
must be agreed separately with the responsible parties. Proposed public rate-limit
metadata retention: seven days. Agree audit retention and legal holds with the
responsible party; linked audit records are also personal information.

There is currently no scheduled retention job or admin deletion action. Do not claim
automatic deletion. Before launch, approve the policy and implement/test a narrowly
scoped deletion workflow and backup-expiry process against the isolated preview DB.
Do not improvise production DELETE statements. Include D1 backups/Time Travel, private
report downloads and employer copies in the policy and information-request workflow.
