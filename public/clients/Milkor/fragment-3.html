05 · Talent Tree search model

Research first. Outreach second.

The value is not the database by itself. The value is a repeatable search system that turns a technical hiring problem into an evidence-based market, a target universe and an approach strategy.

Market-mapping search

Executive-search discipline applied to technical talent.

For niche engineering roles, Talent Tree does not rely on who happens to apply. We identify the technical ecosystems, technologies, teams and adjacent engineering environments most likely to contain the required people, and then work the market deliberately.

A brief becomes a market hypothesis. The market hypothesis becomes a target list. The target list becomes direct conversations.
01

Understand the technical environment

Translate the business need into actual engineering disciplines, platforms, tooling, certification context and must-have versus transferable experience.

02

Map the talent ecosystems

Identify the technical ecosystems, adjacent deep-tech environments and geographic clusters known to train or retain the required skills.

03

Build and rank named profiles

De-duplicate public professional records and rank apparent fit by skill family, industry proximity, technical transferability and location.

04

Approach the passive market

Use relevant, role-specific outreach rather than mass messaging. The objective is to create interest in people who may not be actively applying.

05

Feed market evidence back to HR

Report on response, compensation pressure, relocation friction, title inflation, scarce technologies and where the brief may need recalibration.

Evidence standard

Sanitized, traceable and intentionally conservative.

Public domain only

Milkor public company material, public job advertising, public professional profiles, company pages and research databases. No internal brief or confidential workforce data.

300 total map

The working intelligence map contains 300 named records. Four public-domain internal/transition anchors are used only to understand skill-flow pathways and are excluded from the 296 external addressable count.

Verification discipline

117 profiles were independently revalidated in the latest live sourcing pass. Other research leads are explicitly marked for profile-level verification before any approach.

No availability claims

A mapped profile is not presented as interested, available, affordable, security-clearable or willing to relocate. Those facts require direct candidate validation.
