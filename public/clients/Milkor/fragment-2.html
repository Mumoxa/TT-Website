Scarcity signal

The smallest pools are strategically the most sensitive.

DSP/FPGA/VHDL, composites and controls/autonomy are not areas where a broad advert is likely to create depth. These searches benefit most from a known technical talent universe, targeted technical messaging and careful passive-candidate engagement.

Scale signal

Systems, software and mechanical markets offer more route options.

Systems & integration, GCS/mission software and aeronautical/mechanical talent have larger addressable populations. That creates room to segment by defence exposure, safety-critical environment, location, seniority and exact technology rather than accepting the first available CV.

03 · Geographic intelligence

The Western Cape is the core hunting ground.

The strongest external engineering corridor is concentrated across Stellenbosch, Somerset West/Helderberg and greater Cape Town, with a second substantial defence-tech market around Johannesburg, Midrand, Pretoria and Centurion.
