The operating premise

Start with the talent market, not the advert.

Talent Tree works from a market-mapping model: understand the client’s technical environment, translate it into real skills families, identify where those skills are trained and retained, rank the relevant people, and then approach the market directly.

The objective is not more CVs. It is better control of the search universe.
Important boundary

This is not presented as a Milkor mandate.

No internal Milkor employee has briefed Talent Tree for this exercise. No confidential workforce information, organisation chart, salary data, project staffing or internal succession data has been used. The picture below is what can be assembled from public company material, public job advertising, public professional profiles and research databases alone.

In other words: this is the starting point before calibration.
How Talent Tree would sequence the market

These bands show sourcing priority — not candidate quality.

Before Milkor gives us a live brief, the public market can already be divided into practical search groups. The purpose is to show where we would start, where we would validate one or two variables, and which profiles only become relevant for very specific vacancies.
